The thermal model uses the beta equation and a simplified comparator uncertainty allowance; the actual comparator offset over the chosen low common-mode region, NTC temperature curve, bias accuracy, supply transients and hysteresis must be evaluated on the board. Conservative electrical thresholds can reduce the usable charging temperature window. RT1 must sense the cell through the specified insulating thermal bond.

Physical acceptance work is specified in [bring-up.md](bring-up.md): converter startup/ripple/load steps; capacitor effective capacitance/stability; battery/PCM peak load and cutoff; charge regulation/termination/power-path supplement; thermal lag and open/short NTC response; eFuse, contact reversal/short and ESD; motor startup/flyback; microphone audio quality; SOS/motion wake; BLE range/reconnect; SOC accuracy and runtime; and enclosure/clip/contact/acoustic fit. Record actual conditions and measurements here before changing any of those checks to passed.

The hardware-test firmware keeps the MCU awake. It provides PDM capture and standard BLE advertising, but does not contain an emergency keyword model, phone alert workflow, custom SOS GATT service or sleep/wake implementation. These behaviors and any safety performance claim remain outside the completed CAD/build checks.
